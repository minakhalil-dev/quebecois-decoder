Please read this before using this project. It applies to the code,

## Educational purposes only

This project is a learning tool for Quebec French expressions. It is
NOT an authoritative dictionary, a language course, or professional
translation or language advice.

Definitions and examples here are original, informal writing for
learners. Meanings vary by region, generation, social context, and
tone — the same word can be friendly in one conversation and rude in
another, and a word listed here may be used differently where you
hear it. Do not treat any entry as a definitive or complete
definition.

## Vulgar and familiar language — quoted for education

Some entries are swear words or familiar expressions. They are
included and quoted so learners can *recognise* them, not as an
endorsement and not as a recommendation to use them. The register
label (polite / familiar / vulgar) is a general guide, not a
guarantee that a word is appropriate in any particular situation.

Use any expression at your own discretion and at your own risk.
In particular, do not use vulgar entries at work, in a formal
setting, or with strangers — the register warning in the tool says
the same, and this disclaimer does not change that advice.
